after purchase

the smartest rough part is the chargeback deflection.

this looks vibecoded because it probably is. the clever part is the psychology: the buyer feels like they submitted the dispute inside the brand's system.

what happens

  • the email/order path includes a dispute or chargeback route controlled by the merchant.
  • the form asks for order details, contact details, amount, and reason.
  • the page tells the buyer bank disputes can take up to 90 days.
  • the buyer is asked to use the merchant path before going to the bank.
  • the brain gets the "i already submitted it" feeling, so some buyers stop searching for the bank-dispute path.
