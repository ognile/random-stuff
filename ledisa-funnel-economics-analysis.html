01 / thesis

the first sale opens the monetization sequence.

ledisa's apparent media efficiency is a funnel economics effect. the initial order creates a payment-permission moment, then the buyer moves through one-click offers where each next product is positioned as the missing piece of the same result.

correct model

meta scales when each paid click can produce more immediate cash, more rebill value, more public proof, and fewer lost disputes. the product promise earns the click; the post-purchase system funds the buying.

our current math

we have a real front-end business with limited post-purchase expansion today.

current value = $56 aov + $6 post_purchase_rpv + 75% subscription_take_rate

this supports paid acquisition at one ceiling. the current post-purchase layer carries a small share of the media burden.

ledisa-style math

the buyer enters continuity, then each one-click offer increases immediate cash, trust proof, and dispute control.

scale value = base_subscription + completion_upsells + review_lift + dispute_deflection

this gives the brand more room before the cac ceiling hits.

04 / copy logic

each offer uses the same psychological machine.

the flow is modular. this is why it can be scaled across product categories. the product changes while the underlying persuasion engine stays the same.

1. affirm the buyer you made the right choice. you are on the right path.
2. reveal blocker one hidden issue can slow or hide results.
3. name mechanism bloating, dopamine, nad+, water retention, reward signals.
4. visualize problem simple diagrams, before/after imagery, face/body proof.
5. normalize adding most popular, 93% add, customers like you, limited opportunity.
6. one-click add subscription or bundle anchored by low per-pack price.

05 / our gap

the gap is the spread between their buyer value and ours.

your loom comments matter because they identify the exact places where ledisa extracts more value from the same acquired buyer. every layer gives meta more room to breathe.

gap 01

aov depth

our current aov is $56. the ledisa loom order reaches about $126.95 after the post-purchase sequence.

  • their order can be roughly 2.3x our current aov before rebills.
  • that difference directly expands allowable cac.
  • the delta is created after purchase and on the pdp.
gap 02

post-purchase rpv

our post-purchase app sale rpv is about $6. ledisa has a ladder: unpuff, glp bundle, dopamine, nad+, priority, relax, focus.

  • their first add-on protects the main result.
  • their next offers create new result blockers.
  • their skip traffic is still monetized with exit offers.
gap 03

subscription take rate

our subscription take rate is 75%. the loom frames ledisa as having an almost fully subscription-dominant purchase path.

  • the subscription offer is visually and economically clearer.
  • subscription can live on the first product and on upsells.
  • this affects both immediate aov and expected rebill ltv.
gap 04

public trust score

our trustpilot is 3.8. the target is above 4.4. ledisa is shown around 4.2 in the loom and appears to route happy post-purchase sentiment into review capture.

  • higher rating lifts cold traffic conversion.
  • review volume makes aggressive claims feel more believable to buyers.
  • the review ask happens when the buyer feels welcomed and after support wins.
gap 05

chargeback path

we have beautifier-style chargeback prevention data. ledisa shows a merchant-controlled deflection journey through the disputifier survey in the loom.

  • their buyer is given an internal path before the bank path.
  • the flow captures order number, amount, reason, and phone.
  • this can reduce processor damage and keep more disputes in support.
gap 06

offer-story continuity

their post-purchase products follow one transformation story. every offer extends the original promise into a new consequence.

  • glp-1 creates the appetite result.
  • unpuff makes the result visible.
  • dopamine protects motivation.
  • nad+ protects youth and glow.
06 / scale map

what has to change for us to get the same media freedom.

this is the mechanical interpretation: if we want the same scale behavior, we need the same economic behavior. the flow has to raise post-purchase rpv, subscription ltv, trust conversion, and dispute retention enough that meta can spend more while holding contribution margin.

priority 1: post-purchase rpv

  • current: $6 post-purchase rpv.
  • needed: a ladder where offer 1 is a result-completion product rather than a generic cross-sell.
  • measure: rpv by offer step and total aov.
  • win condition: post-purchase rpv becomes large enough to move allowable cac.

priority 2: subscription clarity

  • current: 75% subscription take rate.
  • needed: make subscription value, cadence, and savings structurally obvious at the offer level.
  • measure: subscription take rate by landing page, ad angle, and pack option.
  • win condition: more buyers enter continuity while front-end conversion holds.

priority 3: trustpilot lift

  • current: 3.8 trustpilot.
  • target: above 4.4.
  • needed: capture high-sentiment moments after purchase and after support wins.
  • measure: rating movement, review request conversion, and pdp conversion lift from rating changes.

priority 4: dispute deflection

  • current: beautifier-style prevention data; deflection journey pending.
  • needed: an internal route for unknown-charge and refund/dispute intent.
  • measure: dispute form starts, support saves, chargeback rate, recovered revenue.
  • win condition: more buyers enter support before bank dispute, and support gets structured data first.
