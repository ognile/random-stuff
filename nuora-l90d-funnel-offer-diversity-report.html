pricing read

capsule has a higher detected unit anchor at $65 and produces a higher AOV ($67.61) with similar CPP ($49.10). gummies has much larger spend, lower AOV ($54.07), and weaker ROAS (1.04x), so the problem is not just page format; gummies needs stronger offer architecture or better route-to-offer matching.

route spend is not offer spend

$75.5k is on articles, listicles, and quizzes, but those are entry routes. their combined performance is 1.34x roas and $44.34 cpp. the offer question is whether the customer sees a different final selection. right now, verified final selections still collapse to the same gummies/capsule subscription ladders.

missing tests

there is almost no funded testing of materially different customer-selectable offers: no separate trial offer, no distinct one-time-vs-subscription framing test, no quiz-only checkout offer, no article-specific bridge offer, and no materially different gummies price ladder at scale. $37.9k is excluded from offer-diversity claims because the final selectable offer is not verified or unavailable.
